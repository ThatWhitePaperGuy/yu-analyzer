# YU Ratio Analyzer (v1.0 BETA)

The **YU Ratio Analyzer** is an automated copywriting tool. It evaluates how much of any marketing copy or white paper is focused on corporate self-interest (**US / the vendor**) versus customer-centric value (**YOU / the audience**).

## The Purpose of the Tool
In B2B marketing, vendors often talk endlessly about themselves: their products, their company history, and their capabilities. The YU Ratio Analyzer helps to encourage audience-first copywriting by analyzing the density of corporate vs. customer language. 
* **The Goal:** A higher **YOU Ratio**, signaling that your content focuses on reader problems, outcomes, and strategies over marketing fluff.

## How to Use the YU Analyzer

This tool is designed to run as a local widget. **No data or text ever leaves your computer.** 

### To Analyze Your Text
1. Open your white paper PDF in your preferred viewer (Acrobat, Preview, or Chrome). Or open your Word file in Word. 
2. Press Command+A in Mac or Cntrl+A in Windows to highlight all the text, then copy all text.
3. Paste the text directly into the **Text to Analyze** box.
4. Enter the **Vendor Name** and any specific **Product Names** to set up the smart-filtering logic.
5. Click **Analyze Text**.

## ⚠ Important Notes

**Approximate Scores Only:** This tool uses hardcoded pattern-matching rather than contextual AI. It provides a sound yardstick for revisions, not an absolute linguistic grade.

**Smart Structure Triggers:** The engine automatically tries to eliminate noise like page numbers, tables of contents, legal boilerplate, back matter like references, and any standard text-formatting markers. 

**The Header Bias Rule:** If the analyzer detects a section that is explicitly labelled as corporate self-interest (such as *“Why Choose Us”* or *“Our Story”*) it flags the entire chunk under that  heading as corporate text.
